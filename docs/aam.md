# Asian American Media

> **Provisional normalization.** The graphs use the 2025 ITU scale. The 2026
> source-year factor uses the project's provisional 6.1-billion Internet-user
> estimate. Raw counts and factors are available in the downloads below.

## Set definition

Confirmed membership in the expanded `aapi-led` union, **Threshold 2**
(qualifying actors plus primary-creator roles), and **USA production required**.
**There is no U.S.-citizenship minimum.** Zero or unknown citizenship does not
exclude an otherwise qualifying work.

The union includes `asian-led-global` and reviewed Asian diaspora or Native
Hawaiian/Pacific Islander identity/descent. Its Asian geographic reference is
**asia-28**, including Macau (`MAC`) and Taiwan (`TWN`). Pacific-only identity
qualifications remain eligible; the Asia geographic table measures Asia-28.
Actors count distinct qualifying people; creators count person/primary-role
pairs. A person can contribute an actor credit and multiple creator-role
credits. Threshold 2 therefore does not necessarily mean two distinct people.

Cohort year comes from the annual measurement inventory. A collection appearing
in multiple annual cohorts has a separate observation in each, with no claim
that their samples are disjoint. Counts are bounded by reviewed metadata;
unreviewed works are listed in the exclusions rather than inferred to qualify.

**181 distinct eligible works; 181 measured cohort entries.** 0 eligible works lack an annual measurement entry.
